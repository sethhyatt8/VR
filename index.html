Pick a color and a shape, then press ORDER.
Squeeze the grip to pick up a brick from any side. It keeps the angle it had on the board. Hold the trigger and squeeze to lift a connected build, then turn your hand to set it down at a new angle. Let go of the grip to drop it. HIDE tucks the order screen away, and PARTS on the left brings it back down over the bin. Match the build turning behind the table: colors and heights have to line up, any turn or mirror counts, and smaller bricks can fill in for a bigger one. Drop a piece in the TOSS bin on your left, or let go away from the table, to throw it away. NEW makes another. R rotates. Hold Shift on desktop to lift a connected build.
